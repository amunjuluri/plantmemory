# Sample logbook photos

Synthetic test images for `POST /api/ingest/logbook`. Both were generated with Gemini
(`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) from invented text; they show no real plant, company or
person.

| File | What it tests |
|---|---|
| `logbook_paper_01.png` | Neat paper-mill shift log, 3 entries, heavy abbreviations, a coffee ring |
| `logbook_pharma_01.png` | Hurried pharma area log, an ink smudge (should come back as `[?]` and be flagged), a crossed-out time, glare |
